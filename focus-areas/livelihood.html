The Employment Challenge

Many capable individuals from backward communities lack access to formal training and skill development. Without marketable skills, they face underemployment and financial instability.

Skill Development & Self-Reliance

Our livelihood programs focus on empowerment rather than dependency. We provide practical, market-relevant training to equip youth and adults with the skills needed to secure employment or start their own micro-enterprises.

  • Vocational training programs (e.g., tailoring, computer literacy)
  • Financial literacy and micro-enterprise workshops
  • Employment placement support and mentorship
