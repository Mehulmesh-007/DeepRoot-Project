Our Story

First Meal Foundation began with a simple belief: no one should be held back by hunger or a lack of opportunity. Witnessing the struggles of marginalized communities in Maharashtra, we recognized that food security is only the first step toward lasting change.

Today, we have grown into a comprehensive social-impact organization. We are dedicated not just to providing nourishment, but to empowering children through education, equipping youth with livelihood skills, and fostering inclusive, self-reliant communities.

Our Vision

To create an equitable society where every individual has access to nutritious food, quality education, and the opportunity to build a sustainable livelihood with dignity.

Our Mission

To implement community-driven programs that eradicate hunger, support holistic child development through education, and provide marginalized groups with the skills necessary for long-term economic independence.

Core Values

The principles that guide our actions and shape our impact.

🤝

Compassion

Acting with deep empathy and respect for every individual we serve.

🔍

Transparency

🌍

Inclusion
