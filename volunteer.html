Why Volunteer With Us?

Volunteers are the backbone of the First Meal Foundation. By joining us, you directly contribute to feeding families, educating children, and empowering individuals toward self-reliance.

Opportunities

  • Food distribution and logistics
  • Mentoring and tutoring children
  • Vocational training instructors
  • Community outreach and mobilization
  • Digital literacy and administrative support

Eligibility

Anyone with a compassionate heart and a willingness to learn can volunteer. Background checks may apply for roles directly interacting with children.
